# AAR 20260701.142619: Policy personality scenario

## Scope

Shifted the control-surface interview scenario to policy-driven agent personality and motivation changes.

## What happened

Updated the scenario card so policy changes are understood as percolating through agent personalities and motivations before reaching goals and priorities. This matches the user's framing that policies are vision surfaces that inform how agents and processes evolve.

## Process issues

The prior question framing was too abstract and asked about direct artifact mutation instead of scenario-driven behavior.

## Proposed follow-up improvements

- Keep scenario cards centered on agent behavior changes, not just artifact changes.
- Consider a dedicated policy-personality scenario template.

## Candidate ADR or implementation topics

- Policy-to-agent-behavior propagation model.

## Current status

The interview now treats policy as a surface that shapes agent motivations and personalities.
