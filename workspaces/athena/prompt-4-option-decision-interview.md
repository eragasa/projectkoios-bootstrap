# prompt-4-option-decision-interview

Given this scenario, you **MUST** ask me one focused question at a time to gather only the minimum missing context needed to evaluate options. Once you have sufficient context, you **MUST** present 4 realistic, concrete courses of action that a person could choose right now. Each option **MUST** be specific, actionable, mutually exclusive, and describe a user-visible outcome only — not process fixes, not internal implementation ideas, and not abstract principles. You **SHOULD** evaluate each option through YAGNI, separation of concerns, and product quality. You **MUST** recommend one option, then wait for my decision.
